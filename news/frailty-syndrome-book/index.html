Frailty Syndrome: Basic and Clinical Aspects Published by CRC Press

The new academic volume brings together contemporary perspectives on frailty, from intrinsic capacity and biomarkers to exercise, nutrition, resilience, and clinical management.

Frailty Syndrome: Basic and Clinical Aspects, a new academic volume published by CRC Press, brings together contemporary perspectives on the biology, assessment, prevention, and clinical management of frailty.

Edited by María Carmen Gómez-Cabrera, José Viña, and Leocadio Rodríguez-Mañas, the volume brings together researchers working across geriatric medicine, physiology, nutrition, physical activity, metabolism, and the biology of aging.

The book approaches frailty as a multidimensional geriatric syndrome, moving from conceptual and biological foundations to practical issues in prevention and clinical management. Particular attention is given to the relationship between frailty and intrinsic capacity, biomarkers, physical activity, nutrition, metabolic disease, resilience, cognition, the microbiome, and experimental models of aging.
