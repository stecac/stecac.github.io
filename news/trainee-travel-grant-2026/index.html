Stefano Cacciatore Receives Institute on Aging Trainee Travel Grant

The award will support his participation in the 2026 Gerontological Society of America Annual Scientific Meeting in National Harbor.

Stefano Cacciatore, MD, received a Trainee Travel Grant from the University of Florida Institute on Aging to support his participation in the 2026 Gerontological Society of America Annual Scientific Meeting.

The GSA Annual Scientific Meeting is one of the major international gatherings in aging research, bringing together researchers, clinicians, educators, and professionals working across gerontology and geriatrics. The 2026 meeting will take place in National Harbor, Maryland, in November.

The grant will support Dr. Cacciatore’s participation in the meeting and the presentation of his ongoing research in aging and functional health.

The award reflects the Institute on Aging’s commitment to supporting early-career investigators and their participation in major scientific meetings.
