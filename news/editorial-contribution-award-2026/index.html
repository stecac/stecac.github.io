Springer Nature Recognizes Stefano Cacciatore for Editorial Contributions

Dr. Cacciatore received a Springer Nature Editorial Contribution Award for the second consecutive year in recognition of his service to BMC Geriatrics.

Springer Nature recognized Stefano Cacciatore with an Editorial Contribution Award in 2026 for his service to BMC Geriatrics. The distinction marked the second consecutive year in which his editorial contributions to the journal were formally recognized.

Editorial work is an important part of the scientific process, supporting rigorous peer review, fair evaluation of research, and the dissemination of evidence relevant to aging and the care of older adults.

The recognition reflects Dr. Cacciatore’s broader involvement in aging research across complementary roles, including original research, clinical geriatrics, and scientific publishing.

He serves as an Editor for BMC Geriatrics, where his editorial activity contributes to the review and development of research across geriatric medicine, gerontology, and healthy aging.
